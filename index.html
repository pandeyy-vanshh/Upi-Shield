Safe Digital Banking & UPI Settlement Guideline

As directed under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) digital payment safety guidelines: merchants and beneficiaries must confirm actual fund credit directly in their official Bank Statement, Bank SMS Alert, or UPI Merchant App before releasing goods or services. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or remote screen access with any caller, and never scan a QR code or approve a collect request to receive funds.
